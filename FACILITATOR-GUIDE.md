# The Lab facilitator guide

## Before people arrive

On your laptop, open the private facilitator link once, then bookmark the [facilitator page](https://brick.btrbot.com/facilitator/). On the projection computer, open the full private big-screen permalink and select **Full screen**. On a browser that already opened the permalink, you can use the [big-screen page](https://brick.btrbot.com/big-screen/).

Before each run in the reusable room, use **Room tools → Session → Reset all…** if there’s a previous run. Leave the projector in the lobby. Participants join by scanning the QR or entering the room code at [The Lab](https://brick.btrbot.com/).

Set out one matching brick kit per person, with spare pieces available. Confirm the piece list and count for your event when you pack the kits. Set seats so each person has a neighbour they can talk to. Check the phone, dashboard and projector in a disposable room.

## Run of show · 40 minutes

| Time | Activity | Facilitator action |
|---:|---|---|
| 4 min | Join and choose an AI app | Participants make both sharing choices here. Watch the join count, then select **Open build plan**. |
| 6 min | Photograph the bricks and get instructions | Say that AI plans can miscount pieces or give inconsistent diagrams. Ask people to build what they can. Watch **have instructions** and **reported no plan**, then add time if the room needs it. Participants save the AI’s first idea and an image as soon as they return. Manual send and fallback choices are under **Need help?**. |
| 5 min | Try the AI’s plan | Select **Start build**. Give everyone time to work with the bricks they have. |
| 3 min | Capture the first builds | Select **Capture builds**. Everyone saves a photo of what they built. The projector fills the photo wall as submissions arrive. |
| 4 min | Reveal, reflect and talk with a neighbour | Select **Start reflection**. The two-minute clock and the paired wall appear together. The phone asks for two ratings and one line about what to change. Select **Start neighbour exchange** and give each person one minute to show their build and describe a change. |
| 6 min | Develop the idea | Select **Start make it yours**. Ask people to take one change back to their AI chat. The bricks were a prototype. The idea can become a game, a ritual, a service, or an experience. |
| 4 min | Make a concept card | Select **Open concept cards**. Everyone saves a name, one-sentence description, image, and a note on what changed. The projector shows incoming cards. Watch the saved card count. |
| 8 min | Compare the cards and debrief | Select **Open final reflection** to show groups of original and final cards. The vote totals appear as people answer. If time allows, open **Room tools → Big screen** for the **Final neighbour exchange**, then select **Follow workshop** to return to the comparison. |

Use the stage strip to jump directly to a phase and the **Next** button to step through the normal sequence. Use **Pause timer**, **Resume timer**, **Restart timer**, or **+1 minute** to adjust the pace. If someone is stuck, ask for the **Builder reference** in their Session settings and open **Participant details** to find them.

## Debrief

Ask people to look at the pairs before you describe a pattern. How similar were the first suggestions? Which builds matched the AI plan? Where did the final ideas diverge?

Pick one pair. Ask the builder what prompted their change. Listen for a missing brick, a neighbour’s observation, a different audience, or an experience the builder wanted to create. Find someone who kept the first idea and ask what made it worth keeping. Invite one observation from the room before showing the results of **What changed most from the AI’s first idea?**

If the room needs an example, use **The Human and Agent Bridge**. The AI first suggested a brick bridge. The builder turned it into a meeting point where a human builds from one side and an AI agent builds from the other. Move to the Magic Button / Centaur / Cyborg slide when you transition to the multiplayer ending.

## Close and document

Open **Room tools → Session** to **Close session** when submissions are in and **Export results** for a private facilitator copy. For the public write-up, open **Room tools → Images** and check **Images approved for gervilabs.com** again just before publishing. Keep the image reference with the published photo and use the Gervi Labs contact route for withdrawal requests. Raw workshop data, including downloads and backup copies, has a maximum retention period of 90 days from joining.

To start a fresh rehearsal, open **Room tools → Session → Reset all…**, check the room name, and type its code in the confirmation dialog. The lobby returns to zero with the same QR and staff links. Previous drafts and pending uploads are cleared when phones reconnect. Export any results you need first.
